Free Google AI key

Importing words and natural audio run on Google's AI. Google gives every account a free key for it. You create it once on Google's site and paste it here — about 2 minutes.

1
Open Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account. Open aistudio.google.com
2
Click “Get API key”, then “Create API key”. Buttons may be worded slightly differently — look for “API key”.
3
Copy the key — a long jumble of letters and numbers starting with AIza.
4
Come back here and paste it below.
